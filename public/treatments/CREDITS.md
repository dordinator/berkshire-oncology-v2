### About the generated hero images

The seven hero images are illustrations created for these page concepts. They
do not show real Berkshire Oncology staff or patients, and their rooms and
equipment are not depictions of any hospital or treatment centre where the
partnership practises. They must not be presented as documentary photography,
evidence of a service or facility, or an example of the exact equipment used
for an individual patient's care.

Keep `Illustrative image` in substantive alt text and in any visible caption
added later. Do not identify a depicted person as a consultant or member of the
practice team. If any generated image is replaced, record the replacement's
source, licence and required attribution here before it is published.

### About the licensed radiotherapy image

**Important:** this linac is at Narayana Multispeciality Hospital, Mysore — not
at any of the partnership's sites. The caption says "of the type used to
deliver external beam radiotherapy" for that reason. Do not reword it to imply
the room, the machine or the hospital belongs to the partnership.

## Future replacement photography

Commissioned or licensed photography could replace the generated hero images
later. Use an aspect ratio of 3:2, avoid identifiable patients and staged
"concerned clinician" stock poses, and do not imply that a third-party room or
piece of equipment belongs to the partnership.

1. **Chemotherapy / immunotherapy day unit** — a treatment chair beside a drip
   stand and an infusion pump, room empty, natural light. Answers "what will
   the room be like?", which is the question patients actually ask.
2. **Brachytherapy afterloader** — the equipment alone, no procedure, no
   patient.
3. **Nuclear medicine therapy room** — the room prepared, no patient.
4. **Radiotherapy planning CT / mask fitting** — equipment and immobilisation
   aids only.
5. **Consulting room** — for the hub, if a warmer opening image is wanted.

Suggested paid sources, per the brief: Getty Images, Adobe Stock, Stocksy and
Alamy. Commissioned photography at a partner location may only be described as
"our" or as a place where the partnership practises once the practice and the
location operator have approved that wording and use.
